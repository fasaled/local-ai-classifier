KITCHEN LAB NOTES - SESSION #47
Restaurant "Tinto y Brasa"
Head Chef: Pedro Ramirez
Sous Chef: Juan Mendez
Date: May 14, 2024
Service: Dinner tasting menu, 14 covers

FOCUS OF THE SESSION
Developing the new spring tasting menu based on the "Andean Smoke" concept. Pedro is leading the creative direction; Juan is handling the technical execution and the brigade coordination.

1. DISH 1 - SMOKED QUINOA WITH GOAT CHEESE FOAM
Pedro proposed replacing the traditional base of barley with white Andean quinoa, cold-smoked for 18 minutes over apple wood. Juan tested three temperatures (60C, 75C and 90C) and identified 75C as the sweet spot, preserving the nuttiness without drying the grain. Plating: a shallow ceramic bowl, foam quenelle on top, finished with a drizzle of chimichurri oil.

2. DISH 2 - LAMB NECK CONFIT WITH FERMENTED AJI
After the failure of last week's attempt, Pedro insisted on a longer confit (9 hours at 72C, vs 6 previously). Juan adjusted the salt cure ratio to 2.5% and added 12g of fermented aji amarillo to the braising liquid. The result: tender, with a clean spicy aftertaste that does not overwhelm the lamb. Approved unanimously by the tasting team.

3. DISH 3 - DESSERT: COCA LEAF SORBET
This is the most experimental dish. Pedro wanted a dessert that reflects the heritage of the Andean region without resorting to cliches. Juan prepared a sorbet base with a coca leaf infusion (legally sourced, certified by the supplier), lime zest and a touch of eucalyptus honey. Served in a frozen stone bowl with a single edible flower.

4. KITCHEN BRIGADE COORDINATION
Juan briefed the four commis on the new plating standards. Each dish has a reference photo pinned above the pass. Pedro emphasised timing: the 14-cover service must run at a 12-minute rhythm between courses, with no exception.
