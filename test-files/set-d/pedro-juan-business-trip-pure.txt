TRIP REPORT - BUSINESS VISIT TO FRANKFURT
Atlas Components GmbH - Q2 2024

Date: April 22-25, 2024
Travellers: Pedro Ramirez (Senior Account Manager) and Juan Mendez (Sales Engineer, EMEA)
Destination: Frankfurt, Germany
Purpose: Annual account review with Atlas Components GmbH and visit to LogiTrans 2024 trade fair

DAY 1 - APRIL 22
Pedro and Juan departed from Madrid-Barajas at 07:35 on flight LH1807, arriving at Frankfurt Airport at 10:10. After checking in at the Maritim Hotel, they were picked up by Klaus Brenner, Procurement Director at Atlas Components.

From 14:00 to 18:00 Pedro led the quarterly business review at Atlas headquarters, presenting the new pricing schedule for FY2024 and discussing the renewal of the master supply agreement. Juan provided technical clarifications regarding the specifications of the new X-Series connectors.

DAY 2 - APRIL 23
Morning session at Atlas focused on the 2025 product roadmap. Pedro negotiated volume discounts for orders exceeding EUR 2M and secured a verbal commitment for a five-year extension, subject to legal review. Juan documented all technical requirements for the engineering team.

In the evening, Pedro attended a working dinner with Klaus Brenner and his procurement team. The dinner was strictly business: agenda items included delivery schedules, penalty clauses and the new sustainability requirements from the German regulator.

DAY 3 - APRIL 24
Pedro and Juan spent the full day at LogiTrans 2024 (Messe Frankfurt, Hall 8). They visited twelve supplier booths, collected product samples and exchanged business cards with representatives from eight European logistics providers. Pedro scheduled follow-up calls with four of them for the week after returning to Madrid.

DAY 4 - APRIL 25
Return flight LH1804, Frankfurt to Madrid, departing 11:20. Juan compiled a 14-page report summarising all supplier contacts, pricing, lead times and technical specifications. Pedro submitted the trip expense claim (EUR 2,847.32) to Finance upon arrival.

OUTCOMES
- Master supply agreement renewal: verbal commitment secured, legal review pending
- 4 qualified leads from LogiTrans 2024 to be developed in May
- Total cost of the trip: EUR 2,847.32 (within budget)
