RESTAURANT "TINTO Y BRASA" - CREATIVE DEVELOPMENT LOG
Session #52
Location: Main kitchen, cold section
Date: June 4, 2024
Present: Pedro Ramirez (Head Chef, owner) and Juan Mendez (Sous Chef, co-owner)

1. EXPERIMENT - FERMENTED COCA LEAF GAZPACHO
Pedro proposed a cold soup for the new summer menu based on fermented coca leaf infusion, blended with heirloom tomatoes from the market. The idea is to deliver a refreshing, slightly bitter opening that resets the palate. Juan prepared three batches with different fermentation times (24h, 48h, 72h) and a control batch without fermentation. The 48-hour version had the best balance: clear coca aroma, no aggressive bitterness, vibrant acidity. Approved by Pedro for the menu.

2. TECHNIQUE - PICKLED QUINOA POP
Juan is developing a technique to make quinoa "pop" like popcorn, then pickle it briefly in citrus brine. The goal is to use it as a garnish for the smoked trout dish. Pedro suggested brushing the popped quinoa with a thin layer of smoked paprika oil for additional aroma. They tested the combination twice; Pedro approved the second version. The texture is crunchy, the flavor profile is nutty-citrus with a smoky finish.

3. PLATING - NEW ARRANGEMENT FOR THE LAMB COURSE
Pedro sketched three new plating arrangements for the lamb neck confit. The final version, sketched on page 14 of the lab notebook, places the lamb on a slate plate with three dots of fermented aji cream, a single pickled shallot, and a small bundle of fresh herbs. Juan will rehearse the plating six times before service to ensure consistency.

4. BUSINESS CONTEXT (brief, captured for the record)
During a short break, Pedro and Juan briefly discussed the business side of the new menu. They agreed that the target food cost for the summer menu should stay below 28%, and that a price increase of approximately 4% is justified by the new signature dishes. They also mentioned, without elaborating, the option of opening a second location in 2025, but no decision was made and the conversation was immediately redirected to the tasting of dish 1.

Pedro clarified that any business decision regarding expansion will be addressed in a separate, dedicated document and is out of scope for this kitchen log.

5. TASTING PANEL
At the end of the session, three invited regular clients tasted dishes 1, 2 and 3. The fermented coca leaf gazpacho received unanimous praise. The pickled quinoa pop was described as "interesting but unconventional" - Pedro and Juan will iterate.
