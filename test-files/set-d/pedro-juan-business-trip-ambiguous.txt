TRIP DOSSIER - SAN SEBASTIAN GASTRO CONGRESS 2024
Distribution Iberia S.L. - New Supplier Programme
Date: May 5-8, 2024
Travellers: Pedro Ramirez (Procurement Director) and Juan Mendez (Product Manager, Specialty Foods)

OBJECTIVE
Attend the San Sebastian Gastronomika congress and conduct B2B meetings with seven potential suppliers from the Basque Country and Navarra, as part of the FY2024 sourcing diversification strategy. No media or marketing activities planned.

ITINERARY OVERVIEW
May 5: Flight IB442 Madrid -> San Sebastian (departure 08:50, arrival 09:55). Hotel check-in at Hotel Maria Cristina. From 14:00, three pre-scheduled B2B meetings at the congress venue (Kursaal) with producers of Idiazabal cheese, txakoli wine and Iberian pork.
May 6: Full day at Kursaal. Pedro attended the round table on "Distribution 4.0" while Juan conducted quality audits at the producer booths. Working lunch with the export director of a Basque cooperative. Evening working dinner with two confirmed suppliers; menu was light, conversation focused on pricing and lead times.
May 7: Final day at the congress. Pedro closed preliminary supply agreements with three new partners. Juan collected technical data sheets and packaging samples for the R&D team.
May 8: Return flight IB447 San Sebastian -> Madrid, departure 13:10. Same-day report compiled and submitted to the CEO.

GUEST PASSES TO COOKING DEMOS
Both Pedro and Juan received courtesy passes to the open-kitchen demonstrations at the congress (including a session by a three-Michelin-star chef). These were attended in a strictly observational capacity; Pedro and Juan did not participate in any cooking, did not handle any kitchen equipment, and did not engage with the chefs in a culinary-collaboration role. The demonstrations were used exclusively as market intelligence: observing trends, identifying innovative techniques worth discussing internally, and validating supplier claims.

FINANCIAL SUMMARY
- Total trip cost: EUR 1,923.40
- Estimated annual margin from the new supplier agreements: EUR 187,000
- ROI projection: 9.7x
- Follow-up calls scheduled for May 13-17

NOTES
Pedro and Juan confirmed that any gastronomic trend observed during the demos will be reported separately as market intelligence, not as menu development work. The kitchen team of Distribution Iberia S.L. is not involved in this trip.
