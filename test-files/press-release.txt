PawPrint Pet Supplies Launches Eco-Friendly Product Line

San Francisco, CA - June 1, 2024 - PawPrint Pet Supplies, a leading provider of premium pet products, announced today the launch of their new eco-friendly product line, "Green Paws."

The Green Paws collection features pet beds made from recycled materials, biodegradable waste bags, and toys crafted from sustainable bamboo. The product line is the result of two years of research and development.

"Our customers have been asking for more sustainable options," said CEO Robert Chen. "We're excited to offer products that are better for the environment while maintaining the quality our customers expect."

The Green Paws line includes:
- Recycled Ocean Plastic Pet Beds
- Plant-Based Biodegradable Waste Bags
- Natural Bamboo Chew Toys
- Organic Cotton Collars and Leashes

Products will be available starting June 15 at pawprint.com and select retail partners. Introductory pricing offers 20% off all Green Paws items through July 31, 2024.

About PawPrint Pet Supplies:
Founded in 2015, PawPrint Pet Supplies serves over 500,000 pet owners nationwide with premium food, toys, and accessories.
