PUBLIC STATEMENT

For immediate publication on the corporate website and distribution to
the media contacts list.

Aurelia Capital Partners
April 9, 2024

Today, Mr. Sebastian Cordero formally communicated to the Chairman of the
Board of Aurelia Capital Partners his decision to step down from his
position as Chief Financial Officer and as a member of the Board of
Directors, effective June 30, 2024. Mr. Cordero cited personal reasons
and the wish to dedicate more time to his family and to the non-profit
foundation he co-founded in 2021.

"Sebastien has been a remarkable colleague and a steady hand through the
challenging markets of the past three years. The Board accepts his
resignation with regret and with deep gratitude for his service," said
Ms. Camille Eickhoff, Chair of the Board. "Sebastien leaves a strong
finance team in place and a balance sheet in the best shape it has been
in a decade. We wish him every success in his next chapter."

The Board has initiated a search for a new Chief Financial Officer,
supported by a leading global executive search firm. A shortlist of
candidates is expected to be reviewed at the Board's regular session in
May, with the appointment to be effective no later than September 30,
2024. Until then, Mr. Cordero will continue in his role to ensure an
orderly transition and to complete the audit of the 2023 financial
statements.

Mr. Cordero joined Aurelia Capital Partners in 2017 as Deputy CFO,
was promoted to CFO in 2019, and was elected to the Board in 2021.
During his tenure, the firm's assets under management grew from EUR
11 billion to EUR 19 billion, and the firm completed three strategic
acquisitions in the Iberian and Benelux markets.

A short biography and a high-resolution photograph of Mr. Cordero are
available in the Press Room section of the corporate website. Members
of the media are kindly asked to address any request for comment to the
Communications Department at the email address below.

About Aurelia Capital Partners
Aurelia Capital Partners is a privately held asset management firm
headquartered in Madrid, Spain, with offices in Lisbon, Brussels, and
Frankfurt. The firm specializes in European mid-market private equity
and infrastructure investments.
