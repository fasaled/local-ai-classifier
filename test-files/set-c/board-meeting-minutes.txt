MINUTES OF THE EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE MEETING
NovaTech Industries, S.A.

Date: April 18, 2024
Time: 10:00 - 13:15
Location: Tower B, 14th floor, Boardroom "Atlas"
Classification: Confidential

ATTENDEES:
- Eleanor Whitfield, President of the Board
- Marcus Delacroix, Vice-President of the Board
- Helena Rojas, Chief Executive Officer
- Daniel Okafor, Chief Financial Officer
- Patricia Lindqvist, Corporate Secretary (minute-taker)

ABSENT:
- None

1. CALL TO ORDER
The President of the Board, Ms. Whitfield, called the meeting to order at 10:05 a.m. and confirmed quorum. She welcomed the Vice-President, Mr. Delacroix, who had joined the session remotely from the Geneva office.

2. APPROVAL OF THE PREVIOUS MINUTES
The minutes of the March 21, 2024 meeting were reviewed and approved without amendments.

3. REVIEW OF Q1 2024 PERFORMANCE
The CFO presented the quarterly results. Revenue grew 7.3% year-on-year, driven by the European segment. The Vice-President raised concerns about margin compression in the APAC region and requested a detailed cost analysis by the next session. The President agreed and tasked the CFO with delivering the report by May 5.

4. STRATEGIC ACQUISITION OF HELIOS ROBOTICS
After an executive session during which Mr. Delacroix disclosed the outcome of the due diligence on Helios Robotics, the committee discussed the proposed acquisition price of EUR 412 million. The President expressed support conditional on a revised earn-out clause. The Vice-President abstained from the final vote citing a personal financial interest disclosed prior to the meeting. The resolution was approved 4-0 with one abstention.

5. APPOINTMENT OF NEW GENERAL COUNSEL
The President proposed the appointment of Mr. Adrian Belluci as General Counsel, effective May 1, 2024, replacing Ms. Stein who will assume the role of Chief Compliance Officer. The Vice-President seconded the motion. Approved unanimously.

6. AMENDMENT TO THE BYLAWS
The committee reviewed proposed amendments to Articles 14 and 22 of the corporate bylaws, addressing the definition of a quorum for virtual sessions and the approval threshold for transactions exceeding EUR 50 million. The President will present the amendments at the next ordinary shareholders' meeting scheduled for June 12.

7. ADJOURNMENT
There being no further business, the President adjourned the meeting at 13:15 p.m. The next executive committee session is scheduled for May 16, 2024.

Respectfully submitted,
Patricia Lindqvist
Corporate Secretary
