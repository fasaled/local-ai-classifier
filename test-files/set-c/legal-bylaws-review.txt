Dear Mr. Quintanilla,

In response to the engagement letter signed on March 28, 2024, we have
reviewed the proposed amendments to the Bylaws of Aconcagua Mining S.A.
("the Company") that the Board of Directors intends to submit for approval
at the next Annual General Meeting of Shareholders, currently scheduled for
June 5, 2024. Our professional fees for this engagement amount to USD 48,500,
as agreed under the retainer.

The proposed amendments affect the following articles of the current Bylaws:

  - Article 9: Definition of "quorum" for sessions of the Board held by
    remote means. The proposed text replaces the prior requirement of
    "physical presence of two-thirds of the Directors" with a broader
    formulation that allows participation through any real-time audiovisual
    medium recognized by the Company's internal regulations.

  - Article 14: Approval threshold for transactions exceeding USD 25
    million. The proposed text lowers the threshold for which a two-thirds
    majority of the Board is required from USD 50 million to USD 25 million,
    and introduces an additional requirement of prior review by the Audit
    Committee for any transaction falling in the new band.

  - Article 22: Term of office and rotation of the Statutory Auditor. The
    proposed text extends the maximum consecutive term from six to nine
    years and clarifies the cooling-off period applicable to the engagement
    partner.

  - Article 27: Introduction of a new section recognizing the authority of
    the Board to delegate certain routine resolutions to an Executive
    Committee, subject to the limits set forth in Article 22 of the Ley de
    Sociedades Comerciales.

Our review focused exclusively on the legal consistency of the proposed
amendments with applicable corporate, securities, and mining law, and on
their enforceability under the current Bylaws. We have not performed, and
have not been engaged to perform, any due diligence on the underlying
business assumptions or financial projections referenced in the recitals of
the proposed text.

Subject to the foregoing, we confirm that the proposed amendments are, in
our opinion, legally valid and enforceable, and that the procedures required
by the current Bylaws for their adoption have been duly observed at the
time of this review.

We will issue our final opinion in the form of a notarial certificate prior
to the convening of the Annual General Meeting. Please do not hesitate to
contact the undersigned should you require any clarification regarding the
contents of this memorandum.
