ANNUAL LETTER TO SHAREHOLDERS
Cordillera Energy Corporation
Fiscal Year 2023

To our Shareholders,

Cordillera Energy Corporation closed fiscal year 2023 with consolidated
revenue of USD 4.82 billion, a 6.1% increase over the previous year, and a
diluted earnings per share of USD 2.14, up from USD 1.87 in FY2022. Operating
cash flow reached USD 1.21 billion, enabling the Board of Directors to
propose a total cash dividend of USD 0.78 per share, payable on May 30, 2024
to shareholders of record as of May 9, 2024.

The audited consolidated financial statements, prepared in accordance with
IFRS and approved by the Audit Committee on February 21, 2024, are included
in the Form 20-F filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on
March 4, 2024.

Performance by segment:

  - Exploration and Production: revenue USD 2.71 billion, adjusted EBITDA
    USD 1.04 billion. Average production of 392,000 barrels of oil
    equivalent per day.

  - Refining and Marketing: revenue USD 1.86 billion, adjusted EBITDA
    USD 218 million. Refinery utilization of 88.4%.

  - Corporate and Eliminations: net expense USD 12 million.

Net debt at year-end stood at USD 3.47 billion, a reduction of USD 410
million, reflecting strong free cash flow generation and the application of
the share buyback program authorized by the Board in November 2022. During
the year, the Company repurchased 6.2 million shares for a total
consideration of USD 198 million.

Capital expenditure for the year totaled USD 1.06 billion, of which 71% was
allocated to upstream development projects in Argentina, Colombia, and
Trinidad and Tobago. The Board has approved a 2024 capex envelope of USD
1.18 billion, focused on the Vaca Muerta unconventional play and the
expansion of the Cartagena refinery hydrocracker unit.

In accordance with Article 24 of the corporate bylaws, the Board has
resolved to submit the following matters to the ordinary Annual General
Meeting of Shareholders, to be held on April 26, 2024 at 10:00 a.m. (local
time) at the Company's registered office:

  1. Approval of the Annual Accounts for FY2023.
  2. Allocation of profit and dividend distribution.
  3. Ratification of the acts of the Board of Directors.
  4. Re-election of Deloitte & Touche LLP as statutory auditor.
  5. Authorization for a new share buyback program of up to USD 300 million.

The full text of the proposed resolutions, the proxy statement, and the
instructions for remote participation are available on the Investor
Relations section of our website.

On behalf of the Board of Directors,

Eduardo Quintanilla
Chief Executive Officer
