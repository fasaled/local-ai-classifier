Helios Robotics Welcomes Dr. Yusuf Akande to Its Board of Directors

COLOGNE, Germany - March 14, 2024 - Helios Robotics GmbH, a leading European
manufacturer of autonomous warehouse systems, today announced the appointment of
Dr. Yusuf Akande to its Board of Directors, effective April 1, 2024. The
announcement was made jointly by the Chairman of the Board, Mr. Henrik
Larsen, and Chief Executive Officer, Ms. Sofia Reinhardt.

The appointment, unanimously endorsed by the Board's Nominations Committee,
expands the Board to nine members and reinforces the company's strategic focus
on the European logistics automation market. A formal resolution to that
effect will be presented at the next Annual General Meeting scheduled for June
20, 2024.

"Dr. Akande brings more than two decades of experience scaling industrial
automation ventures across the EMEA region," said Mr. Larsen. "His track
record in bringing complex robotic platforms from the lab to high-volume
production lines will be invaluable as we enter our next phase of growth."

Dr. Akande joins Helios Robotics from Kestrel Industrial Systems, where he
most recently served as Chief Technology Officer. He holds a Ph.D. in
Mechanical Engineering from ETH Zurich and has been a member of the
Scientific Advisory Board of the European Robotics Forum since 2019.

A high-resolution photograph of Dr. Akande is available upon request.

About Helios Robotics
Helios Robotics GmbH designs and manufactures autonomous mobile robots and
fleet orchestration software for warehouse and manufacturing environments. The
company serves more than 240 customers in 18 countries and has been listed on
the Frankfurt Stock Exchange since 2021.
