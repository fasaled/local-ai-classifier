INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To:         All employees - Miraflores Logistics Center
From:       Ricardo Fonseca, Director of Human Resources
Cc:         Executive Committee
Date:       April 15, 2024
Reference:  HR-MEM-2024-038
Subject:     Update to the Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

Dear colleagues,

Following the audit observations issued by the Group's Internal Audit
function in January 2024, and after review with the Finance Department,
we are updating the Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy, effective
May 1, 2024. The full text of the revised policy is available on the
employee portal under "HR Policies - Finance". A summary of the main
changes is provided below.

1. Per diem rates

The daily per diem for travel within the country is set at USD 65, and
the international per diem is set at USD 110, broken down as follows:

  - Meals:                       USD 30 / USD 50
  - Incidental expenses:         USD 15 / USD 25
  - Ground transportation:       USD 20 / USD 35

Receipts are no longer required for expenses that fall fully within the
per diem. For any expense that exceeds the corresponding component of
the per diem, an itemized receipt is mandatory.

2. Air travel

All flights must be booked through the corporate travel platform. The
platform will default to economy class for flights of less than 6
hours and premium economy for flights of 6 hours or more, unless an
exception is approved in writing by the relevant Vice-President.
Business class remains available, with prior approval, for flights of
more than 9 hours on transcontinental routes.

3. Hotel accommodation

The maximum nightly rate for hotel accommodation is:

  - Tier A cities (Madrid, Mexico City, Sao Paulo, Bogota): USD 220
  - Tier B cities (all other capitals and major hubs): USD 160
  - Tier C cities (all other destinations):        USD 110

Any stay above these limits requires the written approval of the
employee's direct manager and a justification attached to the expense
report.

4. Submission deadlines

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days from the end
of the trip. Reports submitted after this window will be processed in
the next available payroll cycle and may be subject to a 10% reduction
in reimbursable amounts as an administrative penalty, except in cases
of force majeure previously communicated to HR.

5. Corporate credit card

The corporate credit card program is being extended to all employees
with an annual travel budget above USD 8,000. The card will be issued
by Banco Continental and will be linked directly to the expense
management system. Personal use of the corporate card remains
strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.

If you have questions about the application of this policy to a
specific trip, please contact the HR Service Center at
hr.service@miraflores-logistics.example or extension 4012.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Ricardo Fonseca
Director of Human Resources
