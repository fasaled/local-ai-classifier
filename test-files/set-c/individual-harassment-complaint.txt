Subject:     Formal complaint of workplace harassment

Ms. Stein,

I am writing to file a formal complaint regarding the conduct of my
direct supervisor, Mr. Luis Enrique Toledo, Team Leader of the
Accounts Payable section of the Finance Department, over the period
running from approximately October 2023 to the present date. I am
filing this complaint through the internal channel provided for in
the Code of Conduct because I have been unable to resolve the
situation through ordinary line management and because, for reasons
I explain below, I do not consider the union or the workers'
council to be an appropriate venue for a complaint of this personal
nature.

1. Background

I joined the Finance Department in March 2022 as a Junior Accounts
Analyst and was promoted to Senior Accounts Analyst in January 2023.
My direct supervisor since August 2023 is Mr. Luis Enrique Toledo.

2. Facts

Since approximately October 2023, Mr. Toledo has engaged in a
pattern of conduct that, in my view, constitutes workplace
harassment within the meaning of section 4.2 of the Code of Conduct.
The conduct includes, among other behaviors:

  a) Public criticism, in front of peers and in front of other
     teams, of work that I had previously delivered and that had
     been accepted by my previous supervisor. The criticism is
     typically unrelated to the specific document under review and
     focuses on my personal background and on my family
     responsibilities.

  b) Repeated comments about my personal life, including questions
     about my partner, my religious practice, and my decision not
     to attend after-work social events. After I asked him to stop,
     the comments became more frequent and more pointed.

  c) On two occasions, in December 2023 and February 2024, he
     raised his voice at me in an open-plan area of the office, in
     a tone that other colleagues later described to me as
     intimidating. On one of these occasions, a colleague asked me
     afterwards if I was alright.

  d) Allocation of tasks below my grade and below my previous
     responsibilities, and exclusion from meetings at which my
     input had previously been expected, after I raised concerns
     about the timing of a particular invoice batch.

3. Why the internal channel

I have considered, and rejected, the option of raising the matter
through the workers' council or through the trade union delegate
of my section. I do so out of personal conviction, not out of
distrust of those bodies, which I know do valuable work for the
Department as a whole. The matter is, however, of a personal nature
and concerns conduct directed at me individually. I trust the
Office of the Chief Compliance Officer to handle it with the
confidentiality and seriousness that it requires.

4. Request

I respectfully request that the Office open a formal review of
the conduct described above, in accordance with the procedure
set out in section 7 of the Code of Conduct, and that appropriate
measures be taken to ensure that such conduct does not continue.

I am available to provide a detailed account of the incidents
referred to above, in person and in writing, at the convenience
of the Office.
