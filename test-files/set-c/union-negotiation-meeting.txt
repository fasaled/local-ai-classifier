1. PURPOSE OF THE MEETING
The Director of HR opened the session and explained that the purpose was to address the union's formal notice dated April 29, 2024 regarding the planned restructuring of night-shift operations at the Miraflores warehouse, and to initiate a dialogue ahead of the renegotiation of the collective agreement expiring on August 31, 2024.

2. RESTRUCTURING OF NIGHT-SHIFT OPERATIONS
Mr. Salinas presented the union's position, which opposes the elimination of 47 positions and demands alternative measures such as voluntary early retirement, internal relocation, and reduced working hours with full salary compensation for a transition period of 12 months. Ms. Quiroga added that workers have expressed willingness to accept temporary productivity targets in exchange for job stability guarantees.

The company representatives acknowledged the concerns and presented preliminary figures showing an estimated annual saving of EUR 3.1 million under the current plan, and approximately EUR 1.8 million under the union's alternative proposal. A technical sub-committee was formed to evaluate both scenarios in detail and report back within 30 days.

3. RENEGOTIATION OF THE COLLECTIVE AGREEMENT
The Workers' Council and the union delegates jointly submitted a list of nine priority items for the next agreement, including:
   a) A 9% wage increase indexed to inflation
   b) Reduction of the standard working week from 40 to 37.5 hours without salary reduction
   c) Reinforcement of the occupational health and safety committee
   d) Extension of paid leave for employees with dependents
   e) A binding protocol against workplace harassment

Management committed to deliver a formal response by June 1, 2024. Mr. Carranza requested that the response be shared simultaneously with both the Workers' Council and the union representatives, which was agreed.

4. INCIDENT REPORT - MAY 3
The union raised concerns about a workplace accident on May 3 involving a forklift operator in the loading bay. Management confirmed that the internal investigation is ongoing and that the preliminary findings will be presented at the next session.

5. NEXT STEPS
A follow-up meeting was scheduled for June 11, 2024 at 16:00. In the meantime, both parties agreed to maintain open communication channels and refrain from public statements that could undermine the negotiation process.

The meeting was adjourned at 19:30.
