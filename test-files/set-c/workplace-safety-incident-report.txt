INTERNAL INCIDENT INVESTIGATION REPORT
Site: Miraflores Logistics Center, Loading Bay 4
Equipment: Toyota 8FBE20U electric forklift, asset code M-1142
Report author: Esteban Carranza, Health and Safety Coordinator
Reviewed by: Luis Iturriaga, Legal Counsel
Reference: MIR-INC-2024-0503
Classification: Internal - Operations
Date of report: May 6, 2024

1. SUMMARY

On May 3, 2024 at approximately 22:14, a low-speed collision occurred in
Loading Bay 4 between forklift M-1142, operated by a worker with six years
of experience on the same equipment, and a stationary pallet rack. The
operator suffered minor contusions to the right forearm and was transported
to the on-site medical center, where he was evaluated, treated, and
released within 90 minutes. No other workers were in the immediate area at
the time. The pallet rack was displaced 18 cm from its base anchor and has
been taken out of service pending structural inspection.

2. EQUIPMENT FINDINGS

The forklift's onboard diagnostic log (extracted from the CAN bus via the
Toyota I_Site telematics gateway) shows the following sequence in the 30
seconds preceding the impact:

  - 22:13:51 - Travel speed 6.8 km/h, forks raised to 1.4 m, mast tilt 4
    degrees forward.
  - 22:14:02 - Operator released the accelerator pedal. Travel speed
    decreased to 4.1 km/h within 0.4 seconds.
  - 22:14:05 - Operator did not actuate the service brake. Travel speed
    remained 4.1 km/h.
  - 22:14:08 - Audible reversing alarm was not active. A second operator
    in Bay 3 reported that the alarm sounded "weak and intermittent" in
    the minutes before the incident.
  - 22:14:11 - Impact with pallet rack recorded by the onboard shock
    sensor (peak 4.2 g).

The rear combination lamp assembly, which integrates the reversing alarm
buzzer, was last replaced on February 12, 2024 and shows a recorded
service life of 1,184 operating hours. The acoustic output measured at
1.0 m from the lamp housing during the post-incident test was 87 dB(A),
which is below the 95 dB(A) minimum specified in the warehouse's internal
standard WS-04 (acoustics of in-plant mobile equipment).

3. PROBABLE CAUSE

The probable cause of the incident is the reduced audibility of the
reversing alarm, which contributed to the operator's delayed perception of
proximity to the rack when the accelerator was released. The operator
retained visual contact with the load but reported that the perceived
distance to the rack was greater than the actual distance.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  4.1. Replace the reversing alarm unit on forklift M-1142 and verify
       acoustic output against standard WS-04 before returning the unit
       to service.

  4.2. Schedule acoustic output tests for all in-service forklifts in
       the Miraflores fleet within 30 days. Replace any unit below the
       95 dB(A) threshold.

  4.3. Update the pre-shift checklist to include a functional test of
       the reversing alarm by the operator, with a simple "audible at 5
       meters" criterion.

  4.4. Coordinate with the operations planning team to reduce the average
       rack density in Bay 4 during night-shift windows, in line with
       recommendation 7.2 of the 2023 warehouse layout audit.

5. NEXT STEPS

The corrective actions above will be tracked in the operational risk
register. A follow-up report will be issued 60 days after the corrective
actions are closed, and a summary will be shared with the Site Safety
Committee at its next ordinary session.
