PAWPRINT BRAND GUIDELINES 2024

BRAND OVERVIEW:
PawPrint Pet Supplies embodies trust, quality, and care for pets of all kinds. Our brand voice is warm, knowledgeable, and playful while maintaining professionalism.

LOGO USAGE:
- Primary logo: Paw print icon with wordmark
- Minimum size: 1 inch width
- Clear space: Equal to the height of the "P" on all sides
- Do not stretch, rotate, or alter colors

COLOR PALETTE:
Primary Colors:
- Paw Brown: #8B4513 (Logo, headings)
- Trust Blue: #2E86AB (Accents, links)

Secondary Colors:
- Warm White: #FFF8F0 (Backgrounds)
- Soft Gray: #E8E8E8 (Borders)
- Alert Red: #D64545 (Errors only)

FONTS:
- Headlines: Montserrat Bold
- Body: Open Sans Regular
- Accents: Montserrat Italic

TONE OF VOICE:
- Write as a knowledgeable friend
- Use "pet parents" not "pet owners"
- Highlight quality and care in all messaging
- Never use fear tactics or guilt
- Celebrate the joy pets bring to families

DO'S AND DON'TS:
✓ Do show real pets in photos
✓ Do use warm, natural lighting
✓ Do include product benefits
✗ Don't use stock photo dogs in suits
✗ Don't make pets look sad to sell products
✗ Don't overcrowd design
